NO blur, NO smooth vector shapes, NO photorealism, NO outlines thicker than 1 pixel. Give each building charming distinct architectural detail and believable material texture. No giant tower or castle, no readable lettering, no humans. The whole village must be shown without cropping any roof or cart.

The output is illustrative pixel-style art; the prompt's requested 32-color palette is
an art direction, not a measured claim about the generated file. Ground and distant
